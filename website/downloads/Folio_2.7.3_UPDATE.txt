FOLIO 2.7.3 - ANDROID AI WITH PC ACCOUNTING

1. Close Folio and install Folio_Setup_2.7.3.exe over the existing PC version.
2. Install Folio_Android_2.7.3.apk over the existing Android app.
   Do not uninstall either app first. Existing books and sign-in are retained.
3. On the PC, open Mobile sync / Cloud sync. In the salesperson list, select
   Enable full accounting AI for your phone user. Only enable this for trusted
   users: it permits reading and changing the business books through AI.
4. Keep the PC open, online, and cloud sync enabled. Open the star button on
   Android and chat. The phone can use any internet connection.

The supplier activation-code builder is unchanged. Existing activation and
phone pairing are retained. New customers still need their own activation.
The cloud service has been updated; no additional server is required.

EXAMPLES
  Hi bro
  What are my sales this month?
  Export this month's sales in Excel
  Make this month's MIS with per-unit material costs
  Create a customer called Example Traders, phone ...
  Change invoice INV-001 quantity to 20
  Attach invoices and ask to enter them; answer missing-detail questions.

Ordinary report questions return figures inside chat. Excel requests show
Save / share: choose a destination using Android's share sheet. Until saved
or shared, the export is a temporary app file, not a file in Downloads.

Authorized phone AI can use the PC's existing validated accounting tools:
invoices, purchases, receipts/payments, journals, customers, suppliers, items,
ledgers/groups, production/recipes, assets, reports, company/invoice settings,
financial years, permitted cancellations/deletions and reconciliation.
Complete explicit save/change requests execute directly. Say preview to see
a proposal first. Missing or ambiguous details still require an answer.
Passwords, staff permissions, cloud keys and database reset/restore remain
in their dedicated screens. AI does not control the operating system.

If a connection fails, use Check last PC request before repeating an entry.
The same request ID is protected against duplicate saves. Check the PC books
if a request expires. Tool results are removed from the server after receipt;
uncollected results expire after one hour. This is not a cloud backup.

Full-book AI actions and reports require the PC online. Standard mobile
billing and its existing offline queue remain available. Users without full
accounting permission retain restricted phone features.

ATTACHMENTS AND LIMITS
PDF/images up to 4 MB. Excel .xlsx up to 1 MB, one sheet, up to 300 rows;
text CSV/TSV/JSON up to 100 KB. Split larger work into batches.
Up to eight AI tool rounds per chat request; say continue for further work.
Relevant messages, files and requested records are sent to Gemini under
your configured provider plan. AI can misread documents; review saved entries.
Bank-statement reconciliation supports the built-in Bank account only.
MIS unit material costs are estimates at current rates, not full historical
manufacturing costs including allocated labour/power/overheads.
